# Expression Craft · Conditional Transfer Notes

Use when choosing or inspecting an actual expression, alongside the relevant Gate
4–7 constraints. This is a small reusable reasoning asset, not a style library.

## Owner-adopted direction

Design for the audience's understanding cost. Decompose the content into the
relationships that matter before choosing words, graphics, icons, color or motion.
If a meaningful symbol or line requires the viewer to guess its role, label it
directly, choose a more intelligible representation or remove it. Familiarity must
be judged for this audience, not for the designer. Decorative complexity is not craft.

Motion must first serve understanding: reduce matching or inference burden, expose
a supported relationship, or preserve recognition. Aesthetic enrichment can then
add value within that role. Motion-free expression can serve memory, thematic
identity and appropriate emotion; these values are not reducible to reading speed.
Neither density nor sparseness is the target. Make the primary relationship and its
necessary context available without asking the audience to assemble a pile of facts.

## Application and reusable limit

Apply [Editorial Ownership & Guardrail Separation](expression-architecture.md#editorial-ownership--guardrail-separation)
when editing the audience voice. Replace methodology narration and repeated evidence
badges with authored business analysis. Keep a concrete scope or measurement limit
where it changes interpretation; consolidate generic qualifications. Clear presenter
ownership is compatible with candid uncertainty. It does not require stronger claims
or a sparse, text-only design. Judge the rendered reading path, not keyword counts.

The [single Synthesis Candidate](../examples/signature-synthesis-trial/index.html)
uses a prominent qualified conclusion and directly labeled evidence/gap alignment.
The facts, distinctions and gaps supply the expression; no new symbolic language is
needed. Its restrained color reinforces a distinction already explicit in text.

**Candidate insight:** the qualification can be the memory cue itself, rather than
a weaker companion to a dramatic claim. One rendered inspection found this
plausible and semantically compatible; audience memory improvement remains untested.
Do not reuse the headline, columns, palette or uncertainty emphasis when a different
cognitive job calls for another form. A generic “cause unknown” layout is not the asset.
