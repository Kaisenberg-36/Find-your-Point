**Distinctiveness must be earned, not allocated.**

## Definition and value

Signature Expression gives supported meaning a distinctive, perceptible and
well-crafted expression that respects continuity. A Signature Moment is a specific
occurrence, not a required unit in every Scene or Act. Neither requires motion.

Its value can be clearer understanding, durable memory of a core relationship,
grounded emotional resonance, engagement, thematic identity or audience trust.
Choose the value that serves the current Communication Goal; do not turn an
understanding-only briefing into persuasion or invent an audience's emotional needs.
Expected audience effects remain hypotheses until examined, not achieved outcomes.

Ordinary expression need not be broken for a Signature to be worthwhile. It may
communicate accurately while offering less of a relevant memory or emotional
experience. Conversely, correctness, importance and polish alone do not establish a
need for distinctive treatment. Clear ordinary expression is a complete outcome.

## Eligibility reasoning

Start with the existing Cognitive Spine, Primary Signal and audience context, not
an effect. Consider only plausible opportunities: a cognitive turn, an important
relationship, a meaningful contrast, an integration of established evidence or a
grounded thematic/emotional connection. These are prompts, not a taxonomy or quota.

For a promising candidate, reason briefly about:

1. **Audience value:** What should the audience additionally understand, remember
   or experience here, and why does that matter to this Communication Goal?
2. **Ordinary alternative:** What does the clear base expression already achieve?
   What specific additional value is plausible? Do not invent a comprehension
   failure to justify a memory, engagement or aesthetic intention.
3. **Primary Signal:** Does the treatment strengthen the intended relationship or
   compete with it? Can the necessary scope and uncertainty remain perceptible?
4. **Removal:** If replaced by the ordinary alternative, what substantive value
   would be lost? Loss may concern memory, emotion or thematic character, not only
   factual information. “It would look less impressive” is not sufficient by itself.
5. **Implied meaning:** What might the audience infer from prominence, contrast,
   metaphor, timing or interaction even without reading the explanatory wording?
6. **Cost to the experience:** Does the treatment demand attention, matching or
   interpretation that outweighs its intended value? Is the content or the effect
   likely to become the thing remembered?

Use the existing **Meaning × Perceptibility × Craft × Continuity** framework
qualitatively. Meaning supplies a reason; perceptibility makes it available to the
audience; craft makes the treatment coherent; continuity preserves identity and
understanding. It is not a score, formula or compensating trade: craft cannot
outweigh an unsupported claim. Restraint that obscures meaning is not inherently good.

Retain a candidate with its rationale and untested benefit, choose **No Signature
Needed**, or defer the specific unresolved goal/meaning question. Routine expression
choices do not require a new owner checkpoint; changing the communication direction
or adopting an unconfirmed emotional interpretation does. Carry the analysis rather
than asking the user to fill in this list for every node.

## Meaningful expression and semantic truth

Attention determines what is noticed; memory concerns what remains; emotion shapes
how the material is experienced. None establishes evidential strength. A memorable
uncertain explanation must remain memorable *as uncertain*. An emotionally compelling
account must not invent lived experience, consensus, scale of harm or achievement.
Audience trust should be supported by legible reasoning and honest limits, not a
visual performance of authority. Do not assume one tone suits every audience.

Inspect the main expression, not just its disclaimer. It must not convert Proposal
into Outcome, Hypothesis into Fact, Correlation into Causality, reading Sequence into
Progress, or Visual Prominence into an unsupported Ranking or recommendation.
Emphasizing a question or evidence distinction is different from asserting that one
answer is stronger. Essential qualifications stay available with the signal, in
Audience Mode, including any important intermediate State.

If the dominant reading conflicts with Supported Meaning, revise or omit the
treatment. A caveat cannot rescue a contradictory primary expression. If the base
expression itself is misleading, repair that defect before considering Signature;
meeting a basic semantic requirement is not proof of added Signature value.

Forms remain conditional: composition, typography, spatial relationships, annotation,
contrast, deliberate restraint, pacing, interaction or justified motion may help.
This is not a component list or style prescription. Static expression can be
distinctive; a coherent briefing need not repeat one treatment or distribute highlights
evenly. Distinctiveness need not mean novelty relative to every other briefing.

## Interfaces with the established Gates

- **Gate 3 — [Narrative Architecture](narrative-architecture.md):** inherit the
  cognitive job, viewing path and Focus/Return/Synthesis placement. Do not create a
  dramatic turn, new conclusion or extra Scene merely to give an effect a home.
- **Gate 4 — [Expression Architecture](expression-architecture.md):** preserve
  Supported Meaning, Unsupported Reading, attention roles and Semantic Co-presence.
  Distinctiveness can change the form, not silently change the relationship encoded.
- **Gate 5 — [Base Artifact](base-artifact.md):** use existing content and narrative
  identities, evidence links and editable separation from presentation. Do not copy
  facts into decorative text that becomes an independent source of truth.
- **Gate 6 — [Continuity & Motion](continuity-motion.md):** preserve recognition,
  meaning and settled States. A Signature motive does not waive Gate 6's motion
  eligibility. Any proposed motion still needs its concrete cognitive role, ordinary
  static alternative, Unsupported Reading review and motion-free equivalent. Do not
  reopen the prior motion-free continuity result merely to add distinctiveness.
- **Gate 7:** decide where additional audience value is plausible, or unnecessary,
  and hand off that intent. Motion-free Signature and No Signature are both valid.

Use the Expression Feasibility Feedback Loop locally: implementation difficulty to
the renderer; expression friction to the representation; a genuine dependency or
granularity problem to the affected State/Scene; unsupported meaning to its owning
upstream Gate. A disappointing aesthetic result alone does not authorize rewriting
evidence or the Cognitive Spine. Stop a candidate rather than manufacture support.

**Comparison-oriented Scene — No Signature Needed on current evidence.** The existing

No additional audience value has been established for privileging one option; an

**Parallel Evidence Scene — a conditional opportunity, not an adopted design.** Its

make “two causes combined” the memorable takeaway. The prior Trial found no continuity
problem requiring effects, and did not establish a memory deficit or a Signature
benefit. Preserve the current ordinary expression unless a candidate earns additional
value. Neither an animated convergence nor a new visual layout follows from this note.

## Minimal handoff to later implementation

For a retained candidate, keep one compact intent note alongside the affected
narrative node. Reuse the existing records rather than creating a schema or ledger:

- Target Scene/State and content references; the established Primary Signal.
- Intended additional audience value, ordinary alternative and removal rationale.
- Supported Meaning, likely Unsupported Reading and indispensable evidence limits.
- Candidate expression logic and why it fits; attention, identity and co-presence
  requirements that must survive. Keep form provisional where it has not been tried.
- Applicable Gate 6 constraints, inspectable/editable content requirements and the
  ordinary fallback if the treatment fails or is removed.
- Material assumptions and the single most useful question for a bounded Trial.

For No Signature Needed, a brief reason is enough. Do not manufacture a design note.
Later authorized implementation must inspect what the actual expression communicates,
including its uncertainty, rather than treating the intent note as proof. If source
meaning changes, reconsider affected wording and expression; shared keys alone do
not preserve the meaning of a metaphor or paraphrase. No implementation is authorized
by merely recording this handoff.

## Discovery question and validation boundary

The Discovery question was: can one motion-free candidate make a supported core
relationship worth remembering without making its uncertainty less memorable?
Use one cognitive moment and its ordinary alternative, with a stated audience-value
rationale. The parallel-evidence distinction is a possible test subject, not a
mandatory one. Reject the candidate if its only defensible contribution is spectacle
or if it encourages a stronger causal reading. No Signature remains a useful result.

**Candidate knowledge:** remembering a claim while losing its qualification may be a
Signature-specific failure even when both were initially readable. This is a concern
for the next Trial, not an observed failure or a new validated universal principle.
Author inspection can expose misleading expression; it cannot establish durable
audience memory, emotional resonance or trust without suitable audience evidence.

Minimal sanity: distinguish Signature from decoration; permit No Signature; preserve
evidence and meaning; do not equate Signature with motion; keep goals/forms flexible;
do not turn either slice into a template. This establishes a Discovery direction,
not expressive quality, human response or behavioral reliability. Stop here; a Trial
and Gate 8 each require separate authorization.
