# Help improve find-your-point

Use [Issues](https://github.com/Kaisenberg-36/find-your-point/issues) to report a problem or suggest an improvement. Include a small shareable input, your audience and goal, expected versus actual behavior, and your agent/model and operating system.

Do not post private materials, credentials or conversation exports. Synthetic reproductions are welcome. Keep changes focused; changes in meaning require reviewing evidence, dependent wording and the rendered result. Contributions are under the project's MIT License.
